🌊 Introduction to Drainage

Drainage refers to the river system of an area. It includes the main river, its tributaries and the network through which surface water flows.

Drainage System: The network of rivers and streams through which water is collected and carried towards a larger river, lake or sea.
  • Drainage develops according to relief, slope, rock structure and climate.
  • The pattern formed by streams and rivers is called a drainage pattern.
  • Common patterns include dendritic, trellis, rectangular and radial patterns.

🎯 Chapter Focus

  • Learn definitions before memorising examples.
  • Understand the difference between basin and water divide.
  • Remember major Himalayan and Peninsular rivers.
  • Practise east-flowing and west-flowing river comparisons.
